# Mini-Games Suite

A curated collection of **12 fun, responsive mini-games** built with pure HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript. No frameworks, no dependencies, just pure browser-based fun!

## The Games

We've packed 12 different experiences covering action, puzzles, speed, and strategy:

1. **Catch the Ball** - Catch falling items before they escape.
2. **Arrow Game** - Draw your bow and hit moving targets.
3. **Stick Hero** - Stretch your bridge to exactly the right length.
4. **Platformer** - Classic jumping, coin-collecting, and lava-dodging.
5. **Space Shooter** - Blast enemy ships out of the sky.
6. **Brick Breaker** - Classic paddle-and-ball block smashing.
7. **Flappy Square** - Tap to fly through the narrow gaps.
8. **Color Match** - Test your reaction time by matching colors.
9. **Whack A Mole** - Fast-paced mole-smashing action.
10. **Memory Match** - Flip cards to find the matching pairs.
11. **Snake Game** - Classic snake growth and wall-avoiding.
12. **Cookie Clicker** - Click, earn, and build your cookie empire!

## How to Play

These games are 100% web-based and fully responsive! You can play them on any device (Desktop, Tablet, or Mobile).

### Option 1: Play Online (Recommended)
You can access and play the entire suite online via GitHub Pages or any live server hosting this directory:
**[Play Mini-Games Collection Here](https://mini-offline-games.vercel.app/)** *(Replace `#` with your live URL once deployed)*

### Option 2: Play Locally
1. Clone or download this repository to your computer.
2. Open the `index.html` file in any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
3. Click on any game card from the dashboard to start playing instantly!

## Features
- **Cross-Platform:** Works perfectly with both **Mouse/Keyboard** on Desktop and **Touch Controls** on Mobile.
- **Offline Capable:** Progress and high scores are saved locally on your device.
- **Instant Load:** Zero download times, completely client-side architecture.
